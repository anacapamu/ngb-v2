---
title: "Don't Say It Out Loud"
date: 2025-08-26 00:00:00 -0800
tags:
    - poem
---

Shhhh —  
don’t say it out loud.  
If you don’t say it out loud,  
it can’t be true.  

You are not a bad friend.  
You are not a bad daughter.  
You are not a bad mother.  
You are not your thoughts.  

But how long can I keep it in?  

Fear —  
that you’ll see me differently.  
Guilt —  
that I am actually that bad.  
Afraid —  
that if I say it,  
it becomes true.
