This is the first puzzle I’ve ever had where the pieces actually smelled nice right out of the bag. Not sure if it smelled like tea or coffee, but it’s pleasant. The "dots" were easy to put together, but the green and brown pieces took a lot longer. The shapes were unique and fun to work with. Best of all, 100% of the profits go toward building the Maternal Center of Excellence in Sierra Leone, which makes this puzzle worth every penny.
