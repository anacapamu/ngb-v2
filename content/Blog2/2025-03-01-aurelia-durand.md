I haven’t really done jigsaw puzzles as an adult. I started a 3000 or 5000-piece one almost 10 years ago, but my housemate at that time was going through a bipolar episode at the time and messed with it, so I ended up tossing the whole thing.

I got a few puzzles from some neighbors last year and figured, why not just start working on them while I am sick and can't go out?

There’s not too much to say about this one. I liked the artwork, but the pieces were really small. I have to say that it feels good to finally have the time and space to get back into puzzling and to actually finish one.
