500-piece puzzles are starting to feel a bit too easy, so I decided to try a 1000-piece one. I really appreciated that it came with a poster—it meant I didn’t have to keep flipping the box over, and if someone else wanted to join in, they had their own reference too.

The vibrant colors were a fun touch, and the artwork definitely made me crave sweets more than usual.
