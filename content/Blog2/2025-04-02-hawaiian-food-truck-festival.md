This was my first 2000-piece puzzle, and I really enjoyed it. The artwork was fun and full of playful details. It came with a poster, which I found super helpful. In addition, there were some extras too, like a pun list and a hidden Sasquatch to spot.

The finished puzzle was huge. I had to push two basic IKEA tables together just to fit it all. At this rate, I’m not sure I could tackle a 3000-piece one without upgrading my table situation!
