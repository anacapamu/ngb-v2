This jigsaw puzzle was such a blast to put together. The pieces came in quirky, fun shapes like umbrellas, pianos, Effiel Tower, and more. I absolutely loved the artwork (which is by Steve Skelton), and while I was working on it, I kept finding little Easter eggs like Three Blind Mice and the Big Bad Wolf dancing with Little Red Riding Hood.

The puzzle is also made of wood, which gives it a really nice feel. Briefing skimming the Wentworth Wooden Puzzle's online shop, a 500-piece puzzle usually goes for around $120. That’s more than I would want to spend on a puzzle; so, I really got lucky at the local free puzzle library with this find. But if you’re looking to treat yourself or gift someone something special, it’s totally worth the splurge.
